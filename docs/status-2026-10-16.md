## What's in (new this session)
- **Folk:** camps, wanderers, bandits, goblin and orc camps, war bands, 13 encounter types plus springs, fungus and salt.
- **Minds:** needs, traits, memories, reputation, the encounters people turn into (starved, toll, mourner, pilgrim, hunter...).
- **Real items:** packs, camp barrels and crates as real synced containers, eating and drinking actual items.
- **Chores:** water runs, foraging, supply runs, evacuating. **Travellers:** torches, finding fire, water and food.
- **Meetings:** fights, sharing food, merging, joining pilgrims, relighting cold fires, looting.
- **Survival for you:** hunger and thirst, darkness that bites after 5 s, torches that burn out and run out, coins and trading, drifter jobs.
- **Crafting 1+2:** 9 raw materials, carcasses, tools that wear out, X to break items down, tree cutting (loud), salt crusts, spores.
- **Debug:** M map with key, arrows and log; N watch camera with "Doing", carrying, needs, lately, world log and markers; `[ ]` fast-forward (immortal).

## What works
- Everything runs unloaded and loaded with the same rules. The loaded walk spawned bodies at camps, tolls, mourners, pilgrims (6), packs, the bell shrine and wanderers, with no errors.
- Chores happen: 39 water runs and 23 forage trips seen in the hour.
- Travellers go for what they need (sightings: fungus 383, spring 326, cairn 733, own-kind camps 217) and rest at fires.
- Meetings fire: ambushes and packs attacking, food shared with the starving, wanderers merging.
- No more cairn pacing loop since the fix (no repeated line in the logs).

## What's not right yet (fix list, most important first)
1. **The island is dying too fast.** 35% of everyone is gone in one sim hour, and collapses are climbing. Camps lose water and food faster than chores bring it back. Water runs only happen if a spring is within 900 m, and forage trips bring 1 to 4 items each.
   *Fix:* tune the trip yields and how often chores trigger, add a small rain catch into barrels, and let camps send two people at once when it's really low.
2. **Bandits crash: 105 → 29**, most of it in the last 10 minutes. War bands roam far from their fire, live off their packs, and die of thirst or in fights.
   *Fix:* war bands should head home when their packs run dry, and bandits should raid for *supplies* (take from barrels and crates) not just kill.
3. **Ambushes and packs keep jumping the starved.** The weakest people are the easiest targets, which is grim but too frequent.
   *Fix:* lower that pairing, or make the hostile take loot and leave.
4. **Cairns are visited a lot** (733 sightings). Travellers spend a lot of time walking to cairns for light. It's fine but heavy.
   *Fix:* longer NPC torches, or lighting from any traveller who has a lit one (sharing flame).
5. **"Leaving" stalkers (16 goblins) linger** as records after giving up the hunt. They only clear once out of sight.
   *Fix:* let them rejoin a pack or remove them sooner.
6. **Not built yet:** crafting stage 3 (recipes, skill split Ossicraft / Coriaria / Lignifex / Spoliatio, failure and quality), NPC crafting, small magics. Stage C of the minds (values, dilemmas, stealing consequences) is also unbuilt. "Drawn to the flame" is on hold.
7. **Housekeeping:** a lot of work is **uncommitted**, including the whole minds and items redesign, crafting 1+2 and logs. Also, `addons/terrain_3d/bin/~libterrain…dll` was committed by accident and wants a `.gitignore` entry.
